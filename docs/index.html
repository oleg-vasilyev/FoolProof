Telegram bot · Podkidnoy Durak · Free

You play the cards. FoolProof keeps the score.

Sit down with friends and let the bot tell you who was best, with the whole story of the evening and nothing counted by hand. Add it to the group chat, send the line-up once, then mark who opened the game and tap everyone else as they finish. At the end you get two posters about that night, plus any player's record across every game.

Everything, start to finish

Three steps for the whole game

  1. Step 1

    Send the line-up

    Type /game Oleg, Anya, Roma with the names in seating order, clockwise. The bot opens one card in the chat.

  2. Step 2

    Tap who went out

    Start by marking who moved first, press one button per player as each finishes, and end with Confirm. Whoever is left over is the fool. Nothing is typed.

  3. Step 3

    See the results

    /stats draws the chronology, and the awards as well once five games are in. /personal shows one player across every evening they took part in. /next opens another game for the same players.

What it draws

The evening in pictures

The whole evening turns into something to look at and argue over instead of a column of numbers: the best player is named, so is the fool, and nearly everyone at the table earns an award. The posters are easy to read on a phone.

The ideas behind the design

Made for a table of friends

  • Taps, not typing

    Nobody is pulled away from the table to keep score: everything sits in one message with buttons, and a result goes in from a phone, one-handed, in the pause before the next deal. A wrong tap noticed only after Confirm is not final: /reopen brings the last game back as a card, and Back removes the mark.

  • The chat stays private

    In Telegram's default privacy mode a bot is handed only commands and the messages written in reply to its own. The conversation itself never reaches it.

  • Bot went down? Nothing lost

    The card is rebuilt from what was already recorded, so if the bot crashes or is updated in the middle of a game, the evening carries on where it stopped.

  • One player, two names

    Somebody types a name one way at first and another way for the rest of the night. /merge folds both back into a single person. When a whole series went under the wrong name, /replace moves it to the right one.

  • English or Russian

    /language sets the language for that chat, down to the words printed on the posters.

  • Two to ten at the table

    The line-up can change as the session goes on: /next_with brings in somebody who has just joined the table, and /next_without leaves out anyone who has stepped away or wants to skip a game.

FAQ

Questions and answers

Is it free?
Yes. No accounts, no ads, nothing to buy.
Does it read the chat?
No: in Telegram's default privacy mode a bot is handed only commands and the messages written in reply to its own, and the rest of the conversation never reaches it.
What does it store?
The names sent to it, the results of the games played in that chat with each tap recorded against the Telegram id of whoever made it, and the language that chat picked. Nothing else.
Which kind of Durak does it support?
It was designed around Podkidnoy Durak, for two to ten players, but all it records is who went out and who was left holding cards, so any other variant works just as well. It does not deal or referee the game.
Does anything have to be installed?
No. Add the bot to the group and send /game with the names.
Can I run my own copy?
Yes. The whole thing is open source under MIT; it needs Node 24 and has no build step.
