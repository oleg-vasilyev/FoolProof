Telegram bot · Podkidnoy Durak · Free

You play the cards. FoolProof keeps the score.

Play cards with friends and find out who was best that night, without adding anything up by hand. Add the bot to your group chat, send the line-up once, then tap who made the first move and tap each player as they go out. At the end you get two posters about the evening, and any player can ask for a third: their own record across every game they ever played.

One evening

Three steps, one of them on repeat

  1. Step 1

    Send the line-up

    Type /game Oleg, Anya, Roma with the names in seating order, clockwise. The bot opens one card in the chat.

  2. Step 2

    Tap who went out

    Tap who made the first move, then tap each player as they go out, then tap Confirm. Whoever is left is the fool. Nothing gets typed.

  3. Step 3

    Ask how the evening went

    /stats draws the whole evening, game by game, and after five games it draws the awards too. /personal draws one player's record across every evening they ever played. Then /next opens the next game with the same players, and the fool is attacked first, as the rules of Durak say.

What it draws

The evening on a poster

Instead of a column of numbers, the evening becomes something to look at and argue over: who was best, who was the fool, and who earned which award, spread around the table with nine to a poster. All three are comfortable to look at on a phone.

Behind the design

Made for a Friday evening

  • Taps, not typing

    Keeping score never pulls anyone out of the game. The score lives in one message with buttons on it, so a game is marked from a phone, one-handed, between games. Tapped the wrong name? Back undoes it. Noticed only after Confirm? /reopen brings the last game back as a card.

  • Never reads the chat

    In Telegram's default privacy mode a bot receives only commands and replies to its own messages. The conversation never reaches it.

  • Bot went down? Nothing lost

    Every tap is recorded, and the card is rebuilt from that record. If the bot crashes or gets updated in the middle of a game, it comes back with the card exactly where the game was.

  • One player, two names

    Somebody types Anya once and Ania the rest of the night; /merge folds the two back into one person. When a whole evening went under the wrong name, /replace moves it to the right one.

  • English or Russian

    /language sets the language for that chat, down to the words printed on the posters.

  • Two to ten at the table

    The line-up need not stay the same all evening. When somebody joins the table, /next_with adds them to the next game; when somebody steps away or sits a game out, /next_without starts it without them.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. No accounts, no ads, nothing to buy.
Does it read the chat?
No. In Telegram's default privacy mode a bot receives only commands and replies to its own messages; the rest of the conversation never reaches it.
What does it store?
The names sent to it, the results of the games played in that chat with each tap recorded against the Telegram id of whoever made it, and the language that chat picked. Nothing else.
Which Durak does it score?
Podkidnoy, two to ten players. It records the finishing order and the fool; it does not deal cards or referee the game.
Does anything have to be installed?
No. Add the bot to the group and send /game with the names.
Can I run my own copy?
Yes. The whole thing is open source under MIT; it needs Node 24 and has no build step.
